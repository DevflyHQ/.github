# Devfly

**Elevating the digital horizon through technical craftsmanship.**

A multi-disciplinary studio ecosystem where high-level engineering meets creative
storytelling — from enterprise software and immersive gaming to intellectual publishing.

## Business Units
*P&L, client-facing.*
- **[Devfly Digital](https://github.com/devfly-digital)** — B2B / agency & enterprise software, and in-house products.
- **Devfly Interactive** — game development.
- **Devfly Press** — publishing.

## Shared Core
- **Devfly Labs** — R&D / incubator; prototypes that graduate into products.
- **Devfly Creative** — design & narrative service hub, hired by the units.

## Platforms
- **[Ether Platform](https://github.com/Ether-Platform)** — our knowledge-graph substrate
  (a neuro-symbolic *belief graph* where the database is the brain and language models are
  swappable adapters). Incubated in Labs, operated by Digital.
